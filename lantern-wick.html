Lantern Wick

A paper lantern walks home through the hills at night. Its wick burns down as it goes. Fireflies feed the flame, and stone lanterns keep it.

How to walk

Move

Jump

Field notes

Tōrō
Stone lantern. Lights your checkpoint and refills the wick.
Firefly
+30% wick. Another drifts back after 7 seconds.
Spirit stone
Only shows inside your light. Solid all the same.
Sandstone
Gives way 0.4 s after you step on it.
Taiko drum
Land on it to bounce about eight tiles high.
Shoji
Sun and moon screens swap every 1.6 s. Only one set is solid at a time; they flicker just before.
Temple bell
Ring it to open every wooden gate on that path. It stays rung if you are snuffed.
Onsen steam
Rising steam lifts the lantern. Drift sideways to step off at the top.
Torii
The end of each path.
