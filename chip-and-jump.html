You have to reach your ball on foot. Fall in and you restart from the last solid ground you stood on. A ball in water or out of bounds costs one stroke. Red springs launch you and your ball. Roll in too fast and it lips out. Wind pushes the ball while it flies, so watch the flag. Each hole hides three gold stars to jump for, and many hide a power-up box: P for a power shot, M for a magnet cup, U for a mulligan. Swirling portals warp you, or your ball, to their twin. Red pinball bumpers bounce your ball away faster. Fans blow you and your ball upward, and a golden key opens a locked gate. Red laser gates switch on and off, and cracked tiles crumble a moment after you step on them. Walk into a barrel cannon to be fired across a gap, or grab a zipline pole to slide over one. Steam geysers erupt every few seconds: stand on the vent to be blasted up a wall. Grab a balloon's string to float up a tall cliff, or a rope to swing across a gap. On a Coin Rush hole, gold coins line the fairway: grab them for coins. Some holes are played at night with a glowing ball. Hop in a roller coaster car to ride over a big gap. A timer in the corner tracks your best time on every hole. Hold up (or jump) at a ladder to climb it. Step onto a jetty to catch a boat across a lake. On a Low Gravity hole you and your ball float. Every course hides one Golden Ball somewhere high up: find them all!
